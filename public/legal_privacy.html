Privacy Policy

How we protect your identity, safeguard your data, and guarantee zero surveillance on racconmail.eu.

1 Our Data Minimization Philosophy

The best way to protect your personal data is simply not to collect it. Raccon Mail requires only an email username and password to create an account. We do not require your legal name, physical address, telephone number, or government identification.

2 Information We Store

Credentials

Your chosen username and securely salted one-way cryptographic hash of your password. We never see or store plaintext passwords.

Encrypted Mailbox

Inbound and outbound messages, drafts, and attachments delivered to or sent from your account, secured in isolated member stores.

3 Zero Third-Party Telemetry & No Ads Profiling

Unlike legacy advertising-driven email providers, Raccon Mail does NOT scan the text of your emails to build marketing profiles, serve behavioral target ads, or sell audience segments to data brokers. We run zero third-party behavioral trackers across our mailbox applications.

4 Cookies & Local Storage

We use strictly necessary first-party session cookies and browser localStorage exclusively for:

  • Maintaining your secure logged-in authentication session.
  • Remembering your visual interface preference.
  • Offline caching of your postcard drafts to prevent work loss on flaky networks.

5 Global Legal & Regulatory Compliance

European Union (GDPR & ePrivacy)

Full adherence to Regulation (EU) 2016/679. Guaranteed right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), immediate erasure / right to be forgotten (Art. 17), and data portability (Art. 20). User data is hosted strictly in European jurisdictions.

United States (CCPA & CPRA)

We never sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Users maintain the right to know what personal data is processed, the right to delete, and non-discrimination protection under California Civil Code § 1798.100 et seq.

Latin America (Brazil LGPD)

Compliance with Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (Lei 13.709/2018). Transparent, purpose-limited data processing with full member sovereignty and immediate revocation rights.

Asia & Africa (APPI, PIPL & POPIA)

Conforming with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI), China's PIPL, and South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). End-to-end cryptographic boundaries protect against unlawful cross-border interception.
